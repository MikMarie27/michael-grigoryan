## What changed, exactly

- Spelling: *shile* → *Schiele*, *pritty* → *pretty* (then dropped as redundant
  beside "very different"), *piker* → *pinker*, *favorite* → *favourite* to match
  the rest of the site.
- *"I can make your city"* → *"I can make the city"*. "Your" reads as addressing
  the viewer, which is almost certainly not what was meant.
- *nuances* → *small things*. "Nuances" is the right idea but reads oddly in
  English next to "secrets"; his meaning is kept.
- Sentence breaks added. Nothing reordered.

**"The line" was left exactly as he wrote it.** It carries both meanings — the
drawn line and the line he is continuing — and explaining it would ruin it.
